Six questions to ask any LSI

These come from the LSI Evaluation Scorecard. A strong firm answers each one with specific examples and references you can call.

Get the full scorecard

  1. How do you learn what our district wants before designing anything? Look for a structured discovery process that includes teachers and students.
  2. How will you give each school its own scope, milestones, and accountability?
  3. How do you select products, and what financial relationships do you have with vendors? Expect full, written disclosure.
  4. How do you prepare teachers to use the new spaces? Ask for a specific example and its results.
  5. How do you measure success after move-in? Utilization, adoption, practice change, and perception, not test scores.
  6. Which of your past projects is most like ours, and may we call that district?
