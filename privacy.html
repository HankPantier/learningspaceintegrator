Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information learningspaceintegrator.com (“we,” “us”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you request the LSI Toolkit or contact us, we collect your name, work email, organization, role, state, and any optional answers or message you provide.
  • Usage information. We use cookies and similar technologies, including HubSpot analytics, to understand how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed and files downloaded.

How we use it

  • To deliver the resources you request, respond to your messages, and send related follow-up information
  • To send occasional updates about Learning Space Integration if you opt in
  • To understand and improve the site and our resources

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use HubSpot to manage form submissions, email, and analytics. HubSpot processes information on our behalf under its own privacy and security commitments.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any message.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by writing to privacy@learningspaceintegrator.com.

Children

This site is intended for education professionals and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it last changed.
